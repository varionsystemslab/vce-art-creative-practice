Suggested holiday sprint

Four sessions to go from zero to ready-for-Unit-3/4. Tick each off as you go — do them in order, or jump around.

  1. Session 1 — Foundations (≈45 min). Learn the Creative Practice cycle and the three Interpretive Lenses. Everything else builds on this.
  2. Session 2 — Unit 1 (2–3 sessions). Research 3 artists, explore 3 art forms, build your visual diary habit.
  3. Session 3 — Unit 2 (2–3 sessions). Research artists across the 4 required conditions, start a collaborative piece.
  4. Session 4 — Consolidate. Flip every flashcard in the Glossary, then smash the Mixed Quiz until you're scoring 80%+.

The Creative Practice

Every outcome in this study — Unit 1 through Unit 4 — is built around one idea: the Creative Practice, an iterative cycle of four components. There's no fixed order. You can start anywhere and loop back at any point.
